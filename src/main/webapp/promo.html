How to Record this Promo Video

Configure the Settings

Turn on the synthetic background music using the audio button at the bottom. Adjust playback speed if needed.

On Windows, press Win + G to open the Game Bar, or launch OBS Studio to record your screen.

3

Set the capture source to record this browser window, select the area containing the virtual phone, and click record.

4

Restart and Play

Click the Restart button on the controls bar to return to the beginning (0:00) and click Play to start the walkthrough. Let it run until 1:20.
